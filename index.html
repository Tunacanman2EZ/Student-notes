    Backup

    Your notes only live in this browser. A backup file is your safety net: keep it somewhere safe, like a USB stick or a cloud drive. To restore (new computer, cleared browser): click Load a file and pick your backup. Your notes, classes and checkmarks come back.

    Share to the library

    Share your lesson notes with other students. Shared notes go into a public library anyone can read, after an automatic safety check and a review. Never shared: reviews, finals, quizzes, exams, and notes you locked. Emails and phone numbers are replaced with [hidden].
